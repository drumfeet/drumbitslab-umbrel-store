## Application changes

The shared archive budget increases to 2 GiB, with a warning at 1.5 GiB. Raw stock data retains its separate 900 MiB allowance, and the local market storage limits stay unchanged. The dashboard displays the shared and raw allowances separately. Existing cumulative reservations are preserved.
