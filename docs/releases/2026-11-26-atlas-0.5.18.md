# Atlas Almanac Collector 0.5.18

When a provider page is missing, Collector preserves the most recently fetched confirmed dividend history from its cache or its last publication. A confirmed history can include zero payouts. Collector also checks that the 12-month payout count agrees with the latest ex-dividend date. [Ingestion fix](https://github.com/drumfeet/atlas-almanac-collector/pull/76)
