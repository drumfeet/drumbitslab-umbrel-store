# Atlas Almanac Collector 0.5.13

Honor TradingView tool quota cooldowns across bond, stock and company collection
and after restarts. Keep stock work pending during quota blocks, preserve completed
archive work and show the provider retry time in the dashboard. Existing source
validation, collection windows, archive limits, credentials and schedules remain.
