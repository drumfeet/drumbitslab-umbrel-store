# Atlas Almanac Collector 0.5.16

Collector replaces two current snapshots in R2. Phisix and PSEye cover the configured stock universe. TradingView supplies completed daily observations for the 3M, 6M, 1Y, 2Y, 3Y and 5Y government bond maturities. The reports show each yield and its change from the previous close. Existing historical archives, dividends and delivery receipts remain available.

Complete the separate 0.5.15 scheduled stock check. With both production schedules idle and the Atlas readers deployed, seed from the candidate image and verify all six public readbacks before installing. Follow the [snapshot cutover and scheduled verification procedure](https://github.com/drumfeet/atlas-almanac-collector/blob/f61762d165b21867af847e51313280edfdfb96b3/docs/LATEST-SNAPSHOTS.md) and the [private recovery procedure](https://github.com/drumfeet/atlas-almanac-collector/blob/f61762d165b21867af847e51313280edfdfb96b3/docs/BUILD-ONCE-AND-RECOVERY.md).
